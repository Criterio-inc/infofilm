# Research — Leap years

Every number in the film comes from this file (and from `video/src/data.ts`, which cites it).
One section per fact, with its primary source. Never guess: what cannot be verified stays out of the film.

## 0 · General
The opening and closing carry no facts.

## 1.1 · The tropical year
The mean tropical year is about 365.2422 days = 365 d 5 h 48 min 45 s.
Source: IERS Conventions; U.S. Naval Observatory, "The Seasons and the Earth's Orbit".

## 1.2 · The drift
A calendar of 365 whole days falls behind the seasons by about 0.2422 days (≈ ¼ day) every year.
Follows from 1.1.

## 1.3 · Every fourth year
The Julian rule: every year divisible by 4 gets a leap day, 29 February (mean year 365.25 days) — which is about 11 minutes too long.

## 1.4 · The Gregorian correction
Since 1582 (Pope Gregory XIII), century years are leap years only if divisible by 400. Mean calendar year: 365.2425 days.
Source: the bull *Inter gravissimas* (1582).

## 1.5 · Examples
1900 was not a leap year, 2000 was, 2100 will not be. Follows from 1.4.
